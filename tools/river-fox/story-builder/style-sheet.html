Captions

The words Laura says, shown as text on the video so it works with the sound off. "Big words" is one large word at a time in the middle of the picture, for punchy pieces; it is not built yet.

Caption colour

The box behind the words. Brand colours only; the colour of a box can change from video to video.

Headline

A few words at the top of the first shot that say what the video is. Turn off if the opening speaks for itself. With big words there is no headline unless more than 2.5 seconds of silence come before Laura speaks.

use | to break the line

Tighter crop on her face

Sometimes the picture jumps in closer on Laura when she reacts ("Ooh, it smells so nice"). It only does this after the first 4 seconds, when her face is clear, and never as a slow zoom.

130%

Sound

Laura's own voice is always kept, with bumps and rustles cut out. Music is optional.

Making shots

The clips of her making the piece, with no talking, between her spoken lines.

Finish

How the video ends and what picture people see before they press play. There is never an end card. Use the Finish and Cover buttons above the phone to see them.

Images

Extra pictures from the same footage for posts and stories, so one shoot gives you more content.
