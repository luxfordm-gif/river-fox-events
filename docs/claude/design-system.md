## Design system - no one-off values (Matt, 2026-11-08)

Every reusable component and page, the Christmas page included, takes its typography and corners from the design system in `src/index.css`. **Never hard-code a font size, line height, letter-spacing, weight, font family or corner radius in a component.** `src/test/design-system.test.ts` fails the test run if one appears (inline style, Tailwind `text-[15px]` / `leading-*` / `tracking-*` / `font-light` / `rounded-[12px]`, or a raw value in a stylesheet).

- **Text: fourteen styles in four groups, one class per element** (2026-11-08). **Display** `t-display` (home, service and town hero; towns moved from `t-h1` on 2026-11-16 so every hero matches). **Headings** `t-h1` (page title: journal, articles, Contact), `t-h2` (every section title), `t-h3` (titles under a display heading, Our work galleries), `t-h4` (service cards, CTA/form headings, process steps), `t-h5` (card titles, FAQ questions), `t-h6` (photo-tile names). **Quote** `t-quote` (journal pull quotes). **Body** (DM Sans) `t-lead` 18, `t-body` 17 (paragraphs, reviews, form fields), `t-small` 15 (card text, captions), `t-meta` 13.5, `t-label` 13.5 medium (buttons, nav, dropdowns), `t-overline` 11 mono caps. Plus `t-accent` (italic words in a heading), `t-wordmark` / `t-wordmark-lg` (logo), `t-icon`. Weights `--fw-regular` (headings, body), `--fw-medium` (labels, buttons, nav, form fields, wordmark), `--fw-bold` (`<strong>` only). The sizes are the site's pre-restructure ones: a trial "even scale" and bold labels were rejected the same day as too big and shouty, so don't enlarge headings or embolden labels without showing Matt first. Serif is never for long reading.
- **Headings are Regular (400)**; light (300) read too thin in the real Fraunces. Body sizes are the same on phones as desktop. Phones and tablets share one heading scale, H1 to H6: 42 / 36 / 32 / 26 / 22 / 17 (Matt, 2026-11-08). These are the minimums of the size tokens' `clamp()` in `index.css`, so headings grow from there with the screen and never shrink as it widens; never set heading sizes per component or per breakpoint. H1, H2 and H3 get line height 1.1 on phones. Change one heading's size and check the others still step evenly; each size token's comment carries its phone/tablet size, and /style-guide/ shows tablet and phone columns. Each style's size token comment starts with its plain name ("Heading 2 · ..."), which /style-guide/ shows.
- **Body grey** `--text-neutral` is `#494E46` (7.3:1 on the cream background, 6.9:1+ on every tint).
- **Corners: three** (merged 2026-11-08). `rounded-large` / `--radius-large` (16px, 12 on phones: cards, panels, photos, videos, the map), `rounded-small` / `--radius-small` (6px since 2026-11-13, was 4: buttons, form fields, badges, tags), `rounded-full` / `--radius-round` (pills, dots, circles). No other radius. `--radius-panel` (twice Large) is only for the full-width `.rfx-panel` (home About), which starts full width and draws in to its rounded inset on scroll (tablet and up; on phones it stays full width and square, Matt 2026-11-08).
- **Colours: a few named brand colours, everything else is an alias** (renamed 2026-11-08). Surfaces `--background-light` (**the page, sitewide**, and the nav tint), `--background` (cream: review fields, form fields, tinted panels), `--background-warm` (About panel, pricing box), `--background-blush`, `--background-dark` (dark sections, footer); text `--text-primary`, `--text-neutral` (body), `--text-dimmed`, `--text-on-dark`, `--text-journal`; accents `--accent`, `--accent-hover`, `--accent-dark` (italic heading words, stars); plus `--destructive`. The shadcn names (`--card`, `--primary`, `--muted`, `--border`…) are `var()` aliases. Borders and hairlines are `--text-primary` at an opacity (`--hair`, `--divider`, `--hair-strong`; Tailwind `border-hair`, `border-divider`, `border-hair-strong`). **Colour check (2026-11-13):** `src/test/colours.test.ts` fails on inline colour styles, `border-ink/NN` and raw hex in components (allowed: SnowCap's snow shading, the footer's fade mask, the video controls). Use the Tailwind colour names (`text-on-deep`, `text-ink-faint`, `bg-surface-warm`, `text-accent-warm` ...). Before adding a colour, check it isn't within a hair of an existing one. **Christmas** has its own scoped tokens in `src/styles/christmas.css`: `--xmas-page` is one colour for the whole page, hero included (Matt asked for no two-tone page). **Logo and social only, never website colours:** logo pink `#F7EEE9` and logo brown `#5D554B` (the logo, Instagram and Facebook). **Retired, don't use on new work:** `#2B312B` (old ink), `#F1E3DF` (old warm), `#C39083` as an accent (it survives only as the "Warm" logo colourway), the sage accent `#A5C0AD`. The nav and footer use the typed wordmark (`t-wordmark`), not the logo file. Fraunces Light 300 is not used on the website.
- **Shadows, page layers and motion are tokens too (Matt, 2026-11-13).** Before, ~20 shadows, 6 page layers and ~60 timings were hand-written. Shadows: `--shadow-rest`, `-lift`, `-focus`, `-soft`, `-soft-sm`, `-card`, `-menu`, `-panel`, `-float`, `-on-photo`, `-text-photo(-lg)` (Tailwind `shadow-rest` ... `shadow-on-photo`); Christmas-only ones are `--xmas-shadow-*` in `christmas.css`. Page layers, bottom to top: `--layer-float` 90, `-overlay` 95, `-menu` 99, `-nav` 100, `-promo` 150, `-modal` 200, `-top` 300 (Tailwind `z-float` ... `z-top`); small orders inside one component (1, 2, 3) stay plain numbers. Motion: speeds `--motion-instant` 150ms, `-quick` 250, `-base` 400, `-slow` 600, `-reveal` 900, `-long` 1.2s, `-drift` 1.6s, `--motion-stagger` 120ms; curves `--ease-soft` (most movement), `-reveal`, `-glide` (slow photo zoom), `-swing` (in and out), `-shake` (Tailwind `duration-quick`, `ease-soft` ...). Long loops (Instagram strip, About blobs, footer sheen, ticker) keep their own seconds. `src/test/effects.test.ts` fails on a raw shadow, a raw z-index of 50+, or a raw timing between 0.1s and 2s. /style-guide/ shows them all live (E · Effects).
- **Spacing is a system too (2026-11-08).** Never hard-code a gap. "Increase", "decrease" or "next size down" from Matt means change a token's value or switch to the next token, never a one-off number; if nothing fits, add a token in `:root` (comment starts with its plain name) and say so. Tokens: `--section-y` (section padding 64/80/120), `--section-gap` (1.25 x section padding: 80/100/150, the single gap between two sections that share a background; applied automatically by the same-background rule in `index.css`, so don't pad sections by hand. Since the whole site is one background, this is now the gap between most sections), `--gap-heading-text` (20px: **any** heading → the paragraph or label under it, hero H1s and section titles alike, `.heading-to-text`; the single exception is the Christmas video hero, whose script face needs Heading to content. `src/test/heading-gaps.test.ts` fails if text under a heading sets its own margin, Matt 2026-11-13), `--gap-heading-content` (section heading → list/photos/grid/FAQ, `.heading-to-content`), `--gap-text-content` (paragraph → list/grid, `.text-to-content`), `--gap-label-title` (step number/label → title, `.label-to-title`), `--gap-title-body` (card title → its text, `.title-to-text`), `--gap-text-action` (paragraph → the button(s) under it, `.text-to-action`, 32), `--gap-actions` (between buttons, `.actions`, 12), `--gap-columns` (between the halves of a split layout, `.columns-gap`, 64 / 48 phone), `--gap-cards` (between cards in a grid, `.cards-gap`, 40 / 32 phone), `--gap-paragraph` (paragraph → paragraph, `.text-to-text`, 20), `--gap-stack` (small stacked items, `.stack-gap`, 16), `--gap-form` (form title → fields and fields → buttons, `.form-title` / `.form-actions`, 20), `--gap-field` (between fields and before the extras, `.form-grid` / `.form-more`, 12), `--gap-icon` (text to its icon, `.icon-gap`, 8), `--gap-link-list` (`.link-list`, 8), `--gap-eyebrow` (eyebrow label to hero/article title, `.eyebrow-to-title`, 20), `--article-gap` (between every block of a journal article, `.article-block`, 64 / 48 phone), `--article-pad-x`, the `--menu-*` group (nav dropdowns and phone menu), `--gutter`, `--container-max`, `--measure-title` (18ch: section titles wrap sooner, with `text-wrap: balance`, via `.measure-title`) and `--measure-lead` (44ch: the intro under a section title, `.measure-lead`) (Matt, 2026-11-13), plus `--measure-headline` (20ch, page titles), `--measure-card` (36ch, text inside small cards), `--measure-reading` (720px, the journal reading column, Welcome, FAQ answers), `--measure-quote` (820px, pull quotes), `--container-narrow` (1000px, FAQ list, garland options) and `--toast-width`; one-off `max-w-[...]` widths are counted by the spacing ratchet (5 deliberate ones left), `--nav-pad-x` / `--nav-link-gap` (nav side padding and link gap, tighter on 1024-1366 tablets), component padding `--btn-pad-y` / `--btn-pad-x` / `--btn-gap` / `--btn-link-pad` (buttons and text links), `--form-pad` and `--field-pad` (form card and fields), `--card-pad-sm` and `--card-inner-gap` (review cards), `--tag-pad` (tags and badges) (2026-11-13; stylesheet raw spacing 123 → 103), section-level `--reveal-y` / `--reveal-join` / `--reveal-intro-join` (photo reveal rhythm), `--pricing-pad`, `--quote-y`, `--photo-row-inset`, `--gallery-gap-x/-y` and `--gallery-compact-x/-y` (Our work and the Christmas doorway gallery); `--page-pad-bottom` is 40 on phones; every hero's large-screen bottom is `--hero-pad-bottom`. What's left in stylesheets is widget internals (video controls, Our work dock and menu, the floating Christmas card, tiny optical nudges), `--page-pad-top` (space from the top of the page to inner-page titles, nav included; 106px on phones), `--hero-pad-top` (space above the home, service and town hero titles: one setting for all three at every width, never set in a component; on phones it follows `--page-pad-top` so every title starts at the same height). **From tablet up, the home and service heroes (Display headline) instead take their top space from `--hero-gap`**: padding = `--nav-height` + `--hero-gap` - `--hero-ink-trim`, so the visible space from the nav to the headline's letters equals the space from the trust line to the photo below (also `--hero-gap`, at every width, 40 on phones). Measured equal to within 5px at 820-2560 (Matt, 2026-11-13). The nav is exactly `--nav-height` tall: 78px, 72px on phones (it was 87 on tablets; phones went 78 → 64 → 72, Matt 2026-11-13); `--page-pad-top` on phones is 106 so titles stay 34px under it. `src/test/spacing-ratchet.test.ts` fails if one-off spacing values increase, **including Tailwind's own scale (`mt-6`, `gap-3`, `px-4`), counted since 2026-11-13** (452 then); lower its baseline when you remove some. **Token migration (started 2026-11-13):** move those onto token classes a batch at a time with before/after screenshots. Done: heroes, section headers (`.below-heading` sets Heading to content on the block under a heading that sits in its own wrapper). cards and grids (split layouts and card grids; the Journal grid and the phone-only `.rfx-md-stack` 24px gap are still to do). Forms, nav, footer, journal and articles done too (batch 5), and every remaining component (batch 6), all 2026-11-13: **452 → 53**. The 53 left are deliberate component internals (listed in the test). Also: `--gap-media` (photo → card title, `.media-to-title`, 20), `--gap-caption` (photo → its caption, `.photo-caption`, 12), `--gap-photos` (between photos, `.photos-gap`, 12 / 8 phone), `.paragraphs` (paragraph stacks), `.eyebrow-above`, `.divided` / `.divider-gap` (Divider gap above and below a rule), `.bullet-list` (every In short list). `.rfx-md-stack`'s phone stacking gap is `--gap-stacked` (24). /style-guide/ lists every spacing token (P1-P52) with its value at each width; add new ones to the end of `SPACING_TOKENS` in `StyleGuide.tsx` so codes never shift, and give each a `Plain name · note` comment in `:root`.
- **One services section.** Every "our services" block (home, town pages, Balloons, Welcome) is the homepage's `Experiences` component with its rotating photo sets, so it looks the same everywhere; pages may only change its heading, intro and `align`: `centre` (home, Balloons: centred heading only), `stacked` (town pages: centred heading, intro, See our work), `split` (heading left, button right). No rule under the heading; the cards run edge to edge with the page content. The old three-card `ServicesCards` layout was retired on 2026-11-08 - that component is now only the balloon page's photo tiles. Don't build a second services layout. Card photos take their shape from `--service-photo-ratio` (4 / 5, 8 / 9 on phones) and card titles are Heading 4 with the last word on its own line (Matt, 2026-11-17).
- **Service tile cards (Matt, 2026-11-18).** "What I love making" (Children's parties), "Where I start" (Milestones), "What I can build" (Corporate) and the Balloons page's "I'm asked for most" are `ServicesCards`: a flat white card per tile (`--tile-card-fill`, hairline stroke, `--shadow-rest`; no gradient, it read as AI-made, Matt 2026-11-18) with a large square photo on top and the name under it as an `h3` in body text (`t-small`, line height `--tile-card-lh`, padding `--tile-card-pad`). On phones it is a centred swipe row that loops like "Kind words" (the last card sits to the left of the first, Matt 2026-11-22; Embla, switched off from tablet up), about 1.6 cards in view with a neighbour peeking in each side (`--tile-card-width`; raise its 0.62 for fewer in view), with pagination dots (one per two cards); tablet up is a four-across grid showing every tile. Keep tile counts even (12 or 16; Corporate has 10). Names are short, searchable words and fit two lines (check no `h3` overflows); tiles are plain names, never links (Matt, 2026-11-18: a tap that opened an article was unexpected); a tap opens the photo in the shared full-screen `Viewer` (`PhotoViewer.tsx`, as on Our work and the town pages), and on a mouse a card lifts and tilts toward the pointer; photos must be in the Our work gallery. "Sailboards" means the arched backdrop boards. History: a centred one-tile carousel, then a two-across grid of small side-photo cards with "Show more" (PR 172), which Matt found too small, then this. On the four service pages, "Kind words" (`Testimonials` with a per-page order from `src/data/reviewOrder.ts`; the words are never edited) follows "How it works". Known gaps: no wedding photo yet (so no Weddings tile), and "Shimmer walls" borrows the milestone 50th sequin photo on Children's parties until a kids' one is added.
- **Announcement bar (Matt, 2026-11-17, phones only).** A deep pine green strip (the Christmas card's colour) pinned to the top of the screen on every page, staying put as you scroll, with the fixed nav directly under it (it first scrolled away, Matt changed it to sticky the same day), like Wispr Flow's: a short lead-in and a link ending in a chevron (no underline, no button), the whole bar tappable. **All its words are in `src/data/announcementBar.ts`** (a default line, one line per page, no bar on Contact, and the Christmas garland line behind the `christmasMode` setting; the file explains the rules, and a closing "›" in a line shows as the chevron). Edit lines there, never in the component. When a line's link is `/contact/` and the page has its own enquiry form, a tap goes to that form. Every line must stay on one line at 360px wide (about 50 characters with the chevron): don't shrink the text, shorten the line. Component `AnnouncementBar.tsx` (`AnnouncementBarView` is the look, shown at /style-guide/ S23; the default export is the site-wide one mounted in `App.tsx`). Tokens: `--announcement-height` (34), `--announcement-pad-x`, `--fs-announcement` (13px, semibold via `--fw-bold`) and the colour `--announcement-fill` (#052417, deep pine, `155 76% 8%`). The green is the same as `--xmas-pine`; after the Christmas season it may want to go back to a blush (the old `--accent-deep`, #855447). `christmasMode` switches itself off on `christmasEndsOn` (3 January 2027, UK time), so the garland line goes by itself; set `christmasMode` to `false` to end it sooner. A tap sends `announcement_bar_click` with the page path and the line text, after cookie consent; `data-track-skip` stops the page-wide listener also counting it as `cta_click`. The bar itself is a UI element, so the brand book doesn't cover it; only its pine green is a brand colour.
- **Christmas out of season (Matt, 2026-11-22).** One date, `christmasEndsOn` in `src/data/announcementBar.ts` (read through `christmasOn()`), ends the season. From that day the two Christmas pages stay up for next year but the menu and footer links to them go, the home pop-out card goes, the announcement bar returns to its normal lines, the "December dates" FAQ drops out, and both pages show `OFF_SEASON_NOTE` ("Christmas 2026 has been and gone. 2027 garlands open in early November.") in the hero and beside the form, which stays open. The home video banner is separate (`CHRISTMAS_CAMPAIGN`, switched by the workflow, whose push also rebuilds the site so Google sees the change). Each autumn: move `christmasEndsOn` on a year and update `SEASON_YEAR` in `ChristmasDoorways.tsx`.
- **Style guide:** `/style-guide/` (hidden, noindex) shows every token, text style and reusable section live, with a "Gaps on" overlay for spacing notes. Codes like `T5` or `S7·g3` in Matt's notes refer to it. New sections get the next free number (S22 Mission line) so existing codes never shift.
- **Home mission line** (`Mission.tsx`, under the hero): one large sentence, words start faint and darken one by one, driven by the page's scroll (`useScrollProgress`), centred and about 24 characters wide, then a divider halfway into the gap before the services. Its "Book now" button was removed on 2026-11-10 (Matt). **Christmas 2026:** `CHRISTMAS_CAMPAIGN` in `src/pages/Index.tsx` shows the video hero and hides the home title section, so the mission line becomes the page's h1 (`<Mission asTitle />`). It switches itself off on 3 January 2027: `.github/workflows/christmas-switchback.yml` sets it to `false` and pushes to main (Matt, 2026-11-11).
- **FAQ titles** read "FAQs" everywhere except the Christmas page ("Your questions answered.").
- **One section title component (2026-11-13).** Every section title is `<SectionHeading>` and the paragraph under it `<SectionIntro>` (`src/components/rfx/SectionHeading.tsx`): Heading 2 with balanced lines, centred titles at most `--measure-title`, intros at `--measure-lead`, `align` centre / left / md-left / lg-left. A test fails on a hand-built `<h2 className="t-h2">` (exceptions: the mission line and the About counter). Hyphenated words in titles use a non-breaking hyphen (‑) so they never split.
- **One FAQ component.** `FaqSection` (`src/components/rfx/FaqSection.tsx`) on every page; the home questions live in `src/data/homeFaqs.tsx`. The Christmas page's own FAQ padding is `--xmas-faq-top` / `--xmas-faq-bottom` in `christmas.css`.
- **One phone breakpoint.** Phones are up to 767px, tablets from 768px (Tailwind's `md:`); `src/test/breakpoints.test.ts` fails on any other 7xx breakpoint in a stylesheet.
- **No section-title reveal.** A scroll-linked reveal (titles rising and fading in) went live and was removed on 2026-11-08; Matt wasn't sure he liked it. Don't re-add it without asking and showing a recording first.
- **Scroll effects follow the page's own scroll** (`src/hooks/useScrollProgress.ts`), never a timer, and fall back to the finished state with reduced motion or no JavaScript.
- **Need something new?** Add a token in `:root` (and a named style if it's a text role), never a raw value. Stylesheets may use `var(--...)` or neutral values (0, 1, inherit, em, 50%).
- **Fonts:** Fraunces / DM Sans / DM Mono (overlines, since 2026-11-08) load from the Google Fonts `@import` at the top of `index.css`. Google needs axes in alphabetical order (`ital,opsz,wght`); `src/test/fonts.test.ts` checks it. The request was broken until 2026-11-08, so nobody saw the real fonts before then.
