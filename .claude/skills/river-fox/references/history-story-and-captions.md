## Held words are stretched in captions (Matt, 19 Nov 2026: "write it like loooooove")

When Laura draws a word out ("ooh", "love") the caption spells it that way: Oooooh, looove. `story.py` does it itself in word-by-word captions (`stretch_word`, `HOLD` word list, `voiced_run`): the word must be in the list (oh, ooh, ah, wow, love, look, lovely, pretty, yum, yummy, so, no, smell(s), amazing, beautiful, gorgeous, perfect, please, sorted), its transcript length at least 0.45s with real word ends (`transcribe.py` now saves them; text-only transcripts have no ends and never stretch) and a steady voiced sound for at least 0.4s. Extra letters = round((hold - 0.25) / 0.12), 1 to 6, repeated on the stressed vowel. The build prints `held words stretched: ...`; check them in the draft. Switch off with `"held_words": false`. Whole-line (`box`) captions do not stretch. The extra letters then appear one by one in time with her voice (the box grows with each); a long Whisper length of 0.9s or more counts for interjections only (oh, ooh, ah, aww, wow, mmm, hmm, yum), even when the sound has no steady pitch. Added letters are lower case (Ooooooh). Test 19 Nov: Ooh x3, Oh x5 and love x2 were right; "here" (1.4s in the transcript, probably trailing silence) was wrongly stretched, so it is off the list. Add words to `HOLD` as Matt points them out.

## Workflow

1. Copy the clips (never edit originals) to a scratch folder. `ffprobe` each, contact sheet each (`fps=1,scale=120:-1,tile=10x2`), Read them. Note which have sound (`volumedetect`; silent clips have no audio stream).
2. **Ask Matt before sending any audio to OpenAI** (once per job; a yes covers that job). Then: `python3 tools/river-fox/story-builder/transcribe.py <clips_dir> <work_dir> c03.mp4 c10.mp4 ...` for the clips with speech. It writes `<work_dir>/<clip>.wav` and prints words with start times. 3. 4. 5. **Stills:** list the best frames (a contact sheet of candidates every second or two of each clip, with the time written on), then copy `example-stills.json`, edit it and run `python3 tools/river-fox/story-builder/stills.py stills.json`. ## Audio clean-up (every job; Matt, 19 Nov 2026: a sit-down thud, a stray "good" and handling noise all slipped through)

1. **Choose cut points from the sound, not from Whisper.** Whisper's first-word time can be about 0.6s early (it said "So" at 1.3s; the real word was at 1.9s, after a loud sit-down thud). Before cutting a clip that has speech, print its loudness every 0.1s (`ffmpeg -i x.wav -af asetnsamples=1600,astats=metadata=1:reset=1,ametadata=print:key=lavfi.astats.Overall.RMS_level:file=-`), find where the voice really starts (sustained level, not a spike) and start about 0.05s before it. End a few tenths after the last word; never start a segment inside the tail of a previous sentence (that is how a stray "good" got in).
2. **`story.py` replaces bumps with room tone, never with silence (changed 19 Nov 2026).** Matt: dead silence is wrong under music (the background drops out and you notice), and a mute once swallowed real speech. For every segment with `"audio": true` and a `<work>/<clip>.words.json` it finds frames louder than -36 dBFS that no transcribed word covers, then: (a) only windows of 0.55s or less are touched, longer ones are reported `CHECK BY EAR, left alone`; (b) a window is touched only if less than 20% of its frames look voiced (`voiced_share`: real speech measured about 45%, bumps and ambience 0 to 10%); (c) a touched window is crossfaded (40ms) to the clip's own room tone (its quietest half second with no words, `<clip>.room.wav`), so the ambience continues. The report lists `replaced noise with room tone` and `CHECK BY EAR` lines: read both. For long or ambiguous stretches, fix by hand: add `"mute": [[from,to]]` to the segment (it also gets room tone), or cut the picture too when the noise falls in a gap between lines over b-roll (a jump cut is invisible there; never over her face). `"clean_audio": false` switches it off.
3. **Fades:** the whole video's sound fades out over the last 0.3s, and the final mix is normalised to about -15 LUFS (`loudnorm=I=-15:TP=-1.5`) before it is saved.
4. **Check the result:** print the loudness of the joins (the first and last 2 seconds of each segment's `work/segN.mp4`) and confirm the muted stretches read silent (-inf or about -100). Say which clips had noise cut.

## Config (story.json)

`clips` (folder), `work` (folder, holds the .wav files), `out`, `grade`, `segments[]` each with `clip`, `ss` (source start), `d` (output seconds), `speed` (default 1; the clip is read for `d*speed` seconds), `audio` (true if `<work>/<clip>.wav` exists and should be heard), `captions[] {text, from, to}`, `headline {line1, line2, from, to}` or `{kind:"finale", line1, line2, caps, from, to}`; optional `cover {clip, t, line1, line2, out}`. `&nbsp;` between the last two words of a line stops a lone word wrapping.
